# ADR-0034: Scoring v4 grade bands and score gauges

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21 (grade bands revised 2026-11-20 per Issue #788 — see §1)
- Resolves: Issue #758 (parent Epic #728); completes the display-side
  consequences deferred by ADR-0033 ("Grade thresholds redesigned for v4").
  Grade bands further revised by Issue #788 after v0.2.1 acceptance testing
- Related: ADR-0028 / ADR-0029 (v1–v3 contracts, unchanged),
  ADR-0033 (scoring v4 formula, unchanged)

## Context

Scoring v4 (ADR-0033) changed the meaning of `score`: it is now
`baseScore + bonusScore`, each capped at 1,000,000, for a maximum of
2,000,000. Two display layers still assumed the v3-era 1,000,000 ceiling:

- **Grades**: `V6ResultLogic.GradeOf` delegated to
  `V5.ResultView.RankFor` (SS at exactly 1,000,000) and
  `V6SelectDataLogic.GradeOf` to `V5.SelectViewLogic.GradeOf`
  (SS at ≥ 1,000,000). Since every no-miss v4 clear gets
  `bonusScore = 1,000,000` on top of `baseScore`, most v4 plays landed
  at SS.
- **Gauges**: `V6HudLogic.GaugeFillWidth` / `GaugeBestX` and
  `V6ResultLogic.GaugeFillWidth` / `GaugeBestX` normalized by
  `score / 1,000,000` clamped to 1, so any score above 1M pinned the
  gauge full.

Constraints verified against the implementation:

- Grades are **derived, never persisted**: `ScoreStore` keeps only the
  integer `score` per `ScoreKey` (which already carries
  `scoringVersion`), and `ScorePersistence.BestOf` /
  `GManager.BestScoreOf` only surface the **latest** scoring version's
  record. The online leaderboard is likewise scoped to one scoring
  version (the latest by default). So every grade shown in v6 is
  computed from a total score on the 0–2,000,000 scale; the
  base/bonus split is not recoverable from stored data and is not
  needed for grading.
- v1–v3 records still exist under their own `ScoreKey`s and must keep
  their historical grade semantics if ever graded (the version
  parameter exists for exactly this).
- In-play HUD grade used `GameHudLogic.RankText` (same bands as
  `RankFor`), so it pinned at SS as well.

## Decision

### 1. v4 grade bands (0 – 2,000,000) — revised by Issue #788

> **Revision note (Issue #788, 2026-11-20).** The original bands below
> (the doubled v1–v3 thresholds: S ≥ 95%, A ≥ 90%, B ≥ 80%) were rejected
> by v0.2.1 acceptance testing: a single miss erases the no-miss and
> full-combo bonus components and truncates the combo bonus, so every
> play containing a miss scored under ~1.53M and landed on C — B/A were
> unreachable for normal plays. The bands were redesigned from a
> simulated performance distribution (see "Simulation" below). The
> scoring formula itself is unchanged (ADR-0033); grades are derived
> display values, so no `scoringVersion` bump and no record migration
> are needed. Because every new threshold is **lower** than its
> predecessor, re-interpreting stored v4 scores under the new bands can
> only raise a grade, never lower one.

The current v4 bands, chosen from the simulated distribution:

| Grade | v4 threshold | Share of 2,000,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SS | = 2,000,000 (≥) | 100% — perfect all-Perfect full-combo clear only |
| S  | ≥ 1,900,000 | 95% |
| A  | ≥ 1,400,000 | 70% |
| B  | ≥ 1,000,000 | 50% |
| C  | below | — |

#### Simulation

`score = baseScore + bonusScore` (ADR-0033) was evaluated for chart
sizes N ∈ {139, 300, 500, 1000} across judgment mixes (all-Perfect
through all-Bad, including mixed P/G/B profiles) and miss scenarios
(0; a single miss at the start / middle / end; 2–20 misses spread
evenly). `maxCombo` is the longest run between misses (Bad keeps the
combo) and `comboMilestones` counts every 50-combo crossing, matching
`JudgeScoreboard`. Representative results (score, grade under the new
bands):

| Play (N=500 unless noted) | Score | Old bands | New bands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All-Perfect FC | 2,000,000 | SS | SS |
| 90P/10G FC | 1,960,000 | S | S |
| 80P/20G FC | 1,920,000 | S | S |
| 70P/30G FC | 1,880,000 | A | A |
| 50P/50G FC | 1,800,000 | A | A |
| All-Great FC | 1,600,000 | B | **A** |
| 50P/30G/20B FC | 1,720,000 | B | **A** |
| All-Bad FC | 1,200,000 | C | **B** |
| 85P/15G, 1 miss at end | 1,446,411 | C | **A** |
| 85P/15G, 1 miss mid-song | 1,213,700 | C | **B** |
| 85P/15G, 3 misses spread | 1,126,523 | C | **B** |
| 85P/15G, 10 misses (2%) | 967,394 | C | C |
| 50P/30G/20B, 1 miss at end | 1,228,011 | C | **B** |
| 50P/30G/20B, 10 misses | 751,394 | C | C |

Reading of the distribution:

- **Miss plays cap at ≈1.53M** (single late miss + near-perfect
  judgments, N→∞): with `miss > 0` the no-miss bonus (≤5N ratio points)
  and the end bonus (100) are lost entirely and the combo bonus is
  truncated at the break, so `bonusScore` collapses to roughly
  `500,000 / (misses + 1)` for evenly spread misses. S (≥1.9M) and SS
  therefore remain **no-miss-only** grades — the same strictness the
  v1–v3 bands had, where SS meant the literal maximum.
- **A ≥ 1,400,000** sits just below the best achievable miss play
  (~1.41–1.46M across N=139–1000 for one late miss with good
  judgments), so top miss plays and weaker no-miss FCs (all-Great FC =
  1.6M, 50P/30G/20B FC = 1.72M) share the band.
- **B ≥ 1,000,000** covers good-judgment plays up to ~1% misses and
  casual-judgment plays with a single late miss, while ~2% miss rates
  and poor-judgment miss plays fall to C.
- The bands were chosen so typical miss plays spread across B and A
  rather than collapsing into C; S/SS keep their "excellent
  miss-free" meaning. Alternatives considered: A ≥ 1,450,000 (makes
  the single-late-miss grade chart-size dependent — 1.41M on N=139 vs
  1.46M on N=1000 — rejected); B ≥ 1,100,000 (pushes 3–5-miss good
  plays to C — too strict for "normal plays"); S ≥ 1,950,000 (would
  demote 80P/20G FC = 1.92M to A — unnecessary tightening, and it
  would re-grade existing S records downward).

Rationale for the retained choices:

- **SS requires the literal maximum.** A no-miss clear always has
  `bonusScore = 1,000,000`, so `score = baseScore + 1,000,000` and the
  grade ladder for clean plays is effectively driven by the
  judgment-derived base score. Only a perfect all-Perfect full-combo
  run reaches 2,000,000 = SS — the same strictness as v3, where SS
  required exactly 1,000,000. "No miss" alone is never enough.
- **S stays at 95% (≥ 1,900,000).** Miss plays cap at ~1.53M, so S
  remains a no-miss-only grade, and keeping the threshold unchanged
  means existing S/SS records are not re-graded downward.
- **The pre-existing B-boundary divergence stays resolved for v4.**
  The Result/HUD family used 70% (B ≥ 700,000) while the Select family
  used 80% (B ≥ 800,000) under v1–v3 semantics. For v4 a single band
  set is adopted for every surface; the v1–v3 scores keep each
  surface's own historical thresholds unchanged.

### 2. Per-version grade computation

- `V6/Common/V6GradeLogic` (new, pure logic) owns the shared contract:
  `ScoreMaxLegacy` / `ScoreMaxV4`, `ScoreMaxOf(scoringVersion)`,
  `GradeOfV4(score)` and the RankFor-family dispatcher `GradeOf`.
- `V6ResultLogic.GradeOf(score, scoringVersion = Latest)` → "–" for
  score ≤ 0, else `V6GradeLogic.GradeOf` (Result screen, register
  modal, and any RankFor-family surface share one band set).
- `V6SelectDataLogic.GradeOf(score, scoringVersion = Latest)` → "" for
  score ≤ 0, `V6GradeLogic.GradeOfV4` for v4+, and the unchanged
  `V5.SelectViewLogic.GradeOf` bands for v1–v3 (Select list/right
  panel, History, Ranking rows, Result ranking rows).
- Game HUD grade (`V6GameHudView`) uses `V6GradeLogic.GradeOf` with
  `Scoring.LatestVersion` — live plays are always the latest version.
- The default parameter is `Scoring.LatestVersion` because every score
  the UI can display (local best via `ScorePersistence.BestOf`,
  leaderboard rows) is already scoped to the latest scoring version;
  the parameter exists so v1–v3 scores can be graded under their own
  semantics if that ever changes.
- V5 surfaces (`ResultView.RankFor`, `SelectViewLogic.GradeOf`,
  `GameHudLogic.RankText`) are untouched: they are the legacy fallback
  path scheduled for removal under #740 and their bands *are* the
  v1–v3 semantics. The legacy `ResultSceneManager` assessment label,
  which displays the current (always v4) play's score, was switched to
  `V6GradeLogic.GradeOf(score, LatestVersion)` under Issue #788 so no
  surface can show a v4 score under the 1M-scale bands.

### 3. Score gauges (0 – 2,000,000)

Both gauges become a **single total-score rail** normalized by the
latest scoring version's maximum (`V6GradeLogic.ScoreMaxOf`):

- HUD: `V6HudLogic.GaugeFillWidth(score, fillMax)` /
  `GaugeBestX(hiscore, fillMax)` divide by 2,000,000 instead of
  1,000,000. The view passes the full track width (`m.GaugeW`,
  including the "+" segment) so a perfect 2,000,000 fills the rail
  end to end; previously the fill stopped before the "+" segment.
- Result: `V6ResultLogic.GaugeFillWidth` / `GaugeBestX` (and the reveal
  animation in `V6ResultAnimator`, which shares them) divide by
  2,000,000 over the 824 px rail.

Why a single rail and not a base/bonus split fill: the base/bonus
split is not persisted (only the total `score` is stored per
`ScoreKey`), so a split fill could not be reproduced consistently for
the best-score marker. The Result screen already shows the split
numerically (`BonusBreakdownTexts`: base vs combo / milestone / clear
bonuses), so the gauge stays a total-score indicator. The "+" segments
in both gauges remain as the design's top zone, which under the new
mapping corresponds to the ~top 12% of the 2M range.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

- **Change the scoring formula (new `scoringVersion` 5) so misses cost
  less** (e.g. shrink the no-miss component): rejected for this issue —
  the v4 base+bonus split is an owner contract (ADR-0033 §1.1) and a
  formula change would require server recomputation, parity vectors,
  D1 constraints and record separation for every stored score, all to
  fix what is a display-mapping problem. Grade bands are derived
  values; fixing them fixes every surface at once.
- **Grade by `baseScore` alone (bonus as tiebreaker)**: impossible —
  stored scores keep only the total; the split is not persisted and
  adding it would change `ScoreStore`'s format (out of scope).
- **Linear `score / 2` mapped onto the old 1M bands**: equivalent to
  the original doubling, which Issue #788 showed makes miss plays
  unreachable above C.
- **SS at ≥ 1,900,000 or similar "near-perfect" band**: rejected — it
  would award SS to no-miss plays with ~5% judgment loss, breaking the
  v3 precedent that SS meant the literal maximum and making SS common.
- **S reachable by miss plays (S ≤ ~1.5M)**: rejected — miss plays cap
  at ~1.53M, so any S threshold below that would also sweep in
  no-miss plays down to ~1.53M (all-Bad FC = 1.2M territory), making S
  common and erasing the miss-free meaning of the top grades.
- **Piecewise base+bonus gauge fill**: rejected — cannot be computed
  for stored best scores (total only), producing inconsistent fill vs
  marker positions.

## Consequences

- Unity: new `V6GradeLogic`; `V6ResultLogic.GradeOf` /
  `V6SelectDataLogic.GradeOf` are version-aware; HUD grade and both
  gauges use the 2M scale. All grade call sites (Result letter, Result
  modal chip, Select list/right, History, Ranking rows, Result ranking
  rows, live HUD) show v4-consistent values without further changes.
- Web: no grade/rank computation exists (`leaderboard`/`rankings`
  return rank, nickname and score only), so nothing to change; the
  score contract is unaffected.
- Compatibility: v1–v3 scores keep their historical bands through the
  `scoringVersion` parameter; nothing is recomputed or reinterpreted.
  v4 scores keep their stored values; only the grade *labels* derived
  from them change, and only upward (new bands are strictly lower than
  the Issue #758 bands, so no recorded grade can decrease).
- `scoringVersion` stays 4: the score formula, server recomputation,
  D1 constraints and parity vectors are untouched. Web needs no
  change — `leaderboard`/`rankings`/`stats` return rank, nickname and
  raw score only and compute no grade.
- `V6SelectDataLogic.ScoreMax` / `V6ResultLogic.ScoreMax` now mean the
  v4 maximum (2,000,000).

## Verification obligations

- Band boundaries: SS only at 2,000,000; S/A/B at 1.9M/1.4M/1.0M;
  v1–v3 scores keep RankFor / SelectViewLogic.GradeOf bands.
- Distribution-based acceptance (Issue #788): real v4 plays via
  `Scoring.ScoreV4` — perfect FC = 2,000,000 → SS; high-quality FC →
  S; weaker FC → A; a single late miss with good judgments → A;
  a few spread misses → B; ~2% miss rates and poor-judgment miss
  plays → C; for note counts 139/300/500/1000.
- Regression guard: for every score 0–2,000,000 the new grade is never
  lower than the Issue #758 grade (contract-tested).
- Gauges: 1,000,000 maps to half the rail, 2,000,000 to full; values
  above the maximum clamp.
